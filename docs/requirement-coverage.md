# Specification coverage

Source: `../loyalty-platform-specification.json`, 5 October 2026. The approved plan scopes a local, one-location stamp MVP with explicit production dependencies.

The final review adds targeted regressions for reviewed draft revisions, delayed saves, card recovery/redemption during a pause, durable interrupted confirmations, authorization after lock acquisition, concurrent staff login reservations, exact minor-unit amounts and canonical invitation URLs. The revocation test controls the waiting-lock interleaving; hosted PostgreSQL stress remains a rollout check.
